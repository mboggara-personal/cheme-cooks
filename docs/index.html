Recipe · South · Karnataka

Masala Dosa

Fermented rice-and-urad crêpe with a spiced potato filling. Udupi, Karnataka.

VegetarianBreakfastUdupi kitchensB

Also known in the atlas: Mysore masala dosa, benne dosa, set dosa, rava dosa, paper dosa.

Open this dish

Written for the atlas, scaled to your table

  • 120 g rice + 40 g urad dal, soaked 6 hours and ground cold
  • Fermented about 10 hours, until doubled and pleasantly sour
  • Spread thin on a very hot tawa; fill with spiced potato

Tick ingredients off as you prep, then switch to cook mode: one step at a time, with timers.

Nutri-Score B · about 510 kcal per serving

Every dish is scored with the 2017 Nutri-Score algorithm from its own ingredients and cooked weight, using USDA and Indian food tables, and the page shows the working.

Heating · sour and salty

Vata ↓Pitta ↑Kapha ↑

Fermented and sour: warming, which Vata likes; Pitta and Kapha less so. Shown for curiosity, not as medical advice.

From Tamil dosai to Udupi hotels

K. T. Achaya traced dosai in Tamil country to around the 1st century CE; the 12th-century Manasollasa gives a recipe as dosaka; Udupi's restaurants carried the crisp, filled dosa across India.

The ChemE twist

How warm is your kitchen?

Dosa and idli batter is alive. Its bacteria slow down sharply in the cold, which is why the same recipe that rises overnight in Chennai can sit flat in a winter kitchen in Chicago.

The atlas uses the same model in its meal planner, so your soak-and-ferment reminders move to match your kitchen.

about 15 hours
until the batter doubles

The recipe's 10-hour ferment assumes a warm Indian kitchen (about 28 °C); the rate scales by the square-root rule, µ ∝ (T − 5 °C)², as in the atlas planner. Try the full lab →

About the atlas

How it was made

Created by Mohan Boggara and built with AI assistance (Claude) under his direction: recipes written for the atlas, histories summarised from cited sources, and models drawn from published food-science work.

Sources and accuracy

Nutrition from USDA FoodData Central and IFCT 2017; histories link their sources on every dish. Ayurveda and Nutri-Score notes are for information, not medical advice. The map shows India's boundaries as per the Survey of India, simplified.

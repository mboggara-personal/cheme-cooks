About the numbers. Health grades use the Nutri-Score 2017 general-food algorithm (EU/WHO-Europe front-of-pack system), computed per 100 g from ingredient composition (USDA FoodData Central, IFCT 2017) and an estimated cooked yield; daily references are WHO (salt 5 g, free sugars about 50 g) and EU reference intakes. Ayurvedic notes follow the classical rasa–virya–dosha framework and are for curiosity, not medical advice. Origins are tagged documented, traditional or debated. Map boundaries: DataMeet India (CC BY 2.5 IN), simplified.
